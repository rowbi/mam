ABOUT us

Proven Since 1999

Specialists in high-end home renovations, loft conversions and bespoke finishes, we are a family-run company proudly serving London since 1999.

Have a Question?

FAQ

How long does a typical renovation project take?

Project timelines vary by scope, but we provide clear schedules upfront and keep you updated every step of the way. 

Do you handle all aspects of the renovation, including permits and approvals? 

Yes. We manage planning permissions, building regulations, and liaise with local authorities on your behalf. 

Can I stay in my home during the renovation? 

Depending on the work, many clients remain at home with minimal disruption. We’ll advise on what to expect for your specific project. 

How do you ensure quality and craftsmanship? 

We work with trusted tradespeople and maintain strict quality controls to ensure every detail meets our high standards. 

What kind of warranty or guarantee do you offer? 

We stand behind our work with a comprehensive warranty covering workmanship and materials. 

Can you work with my architect or designer? 

Absolutely. We collaborate closely with your team to bring your vision to life, respecting your style and specifications. 

How is payment structured? 

Payments are scheduled in clear milestones aligned with project progress, ensuring transparency and peace of mind.
