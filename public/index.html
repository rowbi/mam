Proven
since 1999

Craft is not a flourish.
It is the whole job.

Specialists in high-end home renovations, loft conversions and bespoke finishes, we are a family-run company proudly serving London since 1999.

With decades of experience and a hands-on approach, we manage multiple projects at a time while maintaining the care, craftsmanship and personal touch that set us apart.

Every material, line and junction is considered as part of the whole.

01How long does a typical renovation project take?

Project timelines vary by scope, but we provide clear schedules upfront and keep you updated every step of the way.

02Do you handle permits and approvals?

Yes. We manage planning permissions, building regulations and liaison with local authorities on your behalf.

03Can I stay in my home during the renovation?

Depending on the work, many clients remain at home with minimal disruption. We’ll advise what to expect for your specific project.

We work with trusted tradespeople and maintain strict quality controls so every detail meets our standards.

05What kind of warranty or guarantee do you offer?

We stand behind our work with a comprehensive warranty covering workmanship and materials.

Absolutely. We collaborate closely with your team to deliver the vision while respecting their specifications.

07How is payment structured?

Payments are scheduled in clear milestones aligned with project progress, keeping the process transparent.
