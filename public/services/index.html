Services

From first idea
to final fit.

We manage the work as one complete piece — bringing structure, craft and finish together under a single, experienced team.

Building / Joinery / Finishing

01

Turnkey renovations

Complete internal reworks, managed end-to-end, for clients who want to reimagine how their home works and feels.

From the first strip-out to the final handover, we coordinate the trades, programme and finish as one joined-up build. The result is a home that feels resolved, not a collection of separate decisions.

02

Finishing & fit-outs

Delivering the final details that others won’t notice — until they are missing.

We bring exacting attention to the last layer of a project: clean junctions, aligned fittings, considered lighting and a finish that holds up to daily life.

03

Loft conversions

Refined new living space, without the need to move.

We turn underused roof space into comfortable, light-filled rooms, balancing structure, insulation, access and proportion around the existing character of your home.

04

Tailored kitchens

Designed around how you cook, live and gather — built for dinner parties into the next decade.

Layouts, cabinetry, worktops and services are brought together as one composition. We focus on intuitive use, durable materials and details that make the room feel quietly special.

05

Custom joinery

Made-to-measure stairs, doors, storage and architectural trims.

Our bespoke joinery is made for the dimensions and rhythm of the room. Each piece is considered as part of the architecture, with practical storage built in from the start.

06

Bespoke windows & doors

Handmade to elevate light, insulation and proportion.

Fitted for period homes and contemporary builds alike, our windows and doors combine sympathetic proportions with dependable modern performance.
